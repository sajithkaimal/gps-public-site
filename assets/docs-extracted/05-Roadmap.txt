GPS — FIVE YEAR STRATEGIC FRAMEWORK
Suggested Activities (2026–2031)
LONG-TERM STRATEGIC OBJECTIVE
Over a five-year horizon, the Global Platform for the South (GPS) seeks to build a globally connected transdisciplinary ecosystem where knowledge, partnership, innovation, technology, and policy are placed at the service of practical solutions and transformative impact for communities across the Global South. Moving beyond an idea-based platform, GPS aims to become an influential institution capable of producing impactful knowledge, reframing narratives, strengthening leadership capacities, building strategic alliances, shaping policy agendas, advancing intellectual and epistemic plurality, and delivering sustainable and scalable initiatives throughout the Global South.
The following activities are designed to progressively transform GPS from an emerging platform into a globally recognized ecosystem connecting knowledge, policy, innovation, mobility, technology, implementation, and strategic cooperation across the Global South and beyond.
