What this site is

gpsouth.org is a public information site. There is no member login or shopping cart. We do not sell personal data.

Cookies and similar storage

Essential. When you choose Accept all or Essential only, we store that choice in a cookie named gps_consent (and a matching browser preference). This lets us stop asking on every visit and honour your decision. It is required for the consent tool itself.

Optional. If you Accept all, we may later load non-essential tools such as anonymous analytics. Those are not active until you accept, and you can change your mind at any time via Cookie settings in the footer.

Essential only. We keep only the consent preference. Optional tools stay off.

Forms

Contact and similar forms send your message to info@gpsouth.org so we can reply. We use the details you provide for that purpose only. Forms do not set a session login cookie.
