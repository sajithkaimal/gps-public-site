Network · Regional Coordination

Rooted in the South. Six hubs and counting.

Headquartered in Kigali, with regional hubs across Africa, Latin America, and the Caribbean — and an architecture built so new hubs join the map, not a redesign.

Extensible by design

The hub architecture — governance, coordination, and this map — is built to add new hubs without redesign. Propose a hub

What a hub does

Each regional hub operates under a Regional Coordinator responsible for regional strategy, partnerships, programming, stakeholder relations, and institutional development. Hubs plug regional universities, governments, enterprises, and communities into the global platform — and carry GPS programs into regional reality.
