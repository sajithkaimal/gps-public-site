The idea behind GPS has deep roots — in a century of Southern cooperation frameworks, and in the everyday experience of researchers, policymakers, entrepreneurs, and artists whose work rarely found its way to one another.

In 2026, that idea took institutional form. Founding documents were adopted, a five-year strategic framework was set, and Kigali was chosen as headquarters — with regional hubs designed from day one across Africa, Latin America, and the Caribbean, and future hubs planned for the Middle East and Asia.

What follows is the arc we are building along. It will grow as GPS grows.
