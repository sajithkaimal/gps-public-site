GPS WEBSITE — SCHEMATIC ARCHITECTURE & WEBMASTER GUIDELINES
Guiding Concept
The GPS website should feel like a living global ecosystem connecting knowledge to action and impact. It Focusing on academia, industry and business, it should not look like the website of a traditional university, NGO, or intergovernmental organization.
Visual inspiration:Stanford — visual elegance and storytellingHarvard — information architectureUC Berkeley — impact orientationOSC — institutional and governance clarity
GPS — unique ecosystem identity
Core GPS Logic:
KNOWLEDGE ↔ INNOVATION ↔ ENTERPRISE ↔ POLICY → IMPACT
The relationship should be presented as interconnected rather than strictly linear.

GPS ECOSYSTEM

GPS

↓

Surrounding and intersecting with this ecosystem:
UNIVERSITIES • GOVERNMENTS • PRIVATE SECTOR • CIVIL SOCIETY • INNOVATORS • COMMUNITIES
The arrows should move in multiple directions, because GPS does not see the relationship as linear. Knowledge can influence policy; policy can stimulate innovation; enterprise can generate new knowledge; communities can identify challenges that drive research and innovation.

The current detailed planning document should remain the GPS content bank and long-term roadmap. It includes numerous future-facing elements—such as the Global South Data & Foresight Platform, Impact Observatory, policy advisory programs, community-based initiatives, research repository, and long-term institutional sustainability strategy—that can be progressively activated as GPS develops.
The table above, however, should become the webmaster's schematic blueprint for the user-facing website. It gives him a clear hierarchy while leaving enough flexibility to develop the visual and technical implementation.
