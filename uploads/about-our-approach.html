Our Approach

Transdisciplinary and trans-sectoral by design. GPS doesn't run a pipeline — it runs an ecosystem, where every part feeds every other.

The GPS ecosystem

Four pillars. Six kinds of actors. Every direction.

Knowledge can influence policy. Policy can stimulate innovation. Enterprise can generate new knowledge. Communities can identify the challenges that drive research. The relationships are never linear — and the platform is built to keep them moving.

How it works

From lived reality to measurable change.

Start

Real-world situations

Contextual realities and lived, observed, carefully analysed experience.

Generate

Knowledge

Research, plural knowledge systems, and creative and scientific innovation.

Translate

Policy & ventures

Evidence informs policy design; ideas become programs, ventures, and solutions.

Deliver

Impact

Action that generates results, improves lives, and strengthens societies.

This is the KPI framework — Knowledge, Policy, Impact — GPS's diagnostic platform, policy interface, and implementation bridge in one.

Transdisciplinary

GPS organizes action across interconnected domains — methodology, epistemology, culture, socio-economic systems, technology, and environment — rather than within academic silos. Research agendas are framed by problems, not disciplines.

Trans-sectoral

Universities, governments, businesses, innovators, researchers, civil society, artists, and communities co-design and implement together. The platform's job is to hold these actors in productive, equitable relationship.

Context-responsive

Locally grounded research aligned to priorities defined by Global South states and communities — with solutions that are historically grounded and socially embedded, engaging lived experience while contributing to global conversations.
