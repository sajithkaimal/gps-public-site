Network · Diaspora

The Diaspora Network

Scholars, entrepreneurs, artists, and professionals of the Global South — wherever they live. The diaspora is structural to GPS, connected back to every hub.

Map loading — engagement pathways below.

Every hub is a point of contact for diaspora communities. The five engagement pathways below open ahead of Kigali 2027.

From 2027 · Diaspora & Global Networks Initiative

Five ways the diaspora plugs in.

01

Diaspora partnerships

Institutional links with diaspora associations, professional networks, and cultural organizations.

02

Visiting experts & fellows

Structured visits bringing diaspora expertise into hubs, labs, and universities.

03

Transnational mentorship

Diaspora professionals mentoring young leaders and founders across the network.

04

Global networking platforms

Digital and in-person spaces connecting diaspora communities to home-region priorities.

05

Collaborative research & innovation

Joint projects pairing diaspora researchers and builders with hub-based teams.
