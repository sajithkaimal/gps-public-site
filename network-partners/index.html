01

Hybrid Technics (Pvt.) Ltd.

Founder / Executive: Mohsin M. Syed (Syed Muhammad Mohsin)
Base / Reach: Pakistan · Industrial, aviation and technical-education sectors

Leadership profile

A Pakistani engineer, industrialist and aviation entrepreneur whose career bridges private enterprise, industrial development and public-sector reform. An electrical and electronics engineer and licensed commercial pilot, he founded Hybrid Technics to connect technology, manufacturing and workforce development.

Principal activities

Hybrid Technics operates in industrial automation, aviation services, pilot training, manufacturing and technical education. Its work supports stronger links between industry and vocational training, promotes technology transfer and practical skills, and contributes to industrial capacity through public–private cooperation.

GPS linkages

GPS can connect Hybrid Technics with its Knowledge & Innovation and Skills & Workforce agendas through industry–university collaboration, technical and vocational education, technology transfer, applied research, and public–private innovation initiatives.

02

Beyond Africa Group

Founder / Executive: Paulo Pan
Base / Reach: Brazil, Rwanda and Africa-wide networks

Leadership profile

A Brazilian business leader and senior adviser on Africa with extensive experience in international relations and development projects. He founded Beyond Africa Group as a business, investment and strategic-relations ecosystem connecting Brazilian capabilities with African institutions, markets and development priorities.

Principal activities

The Group facilitates partnerships among companies, governments, diplomatic missions and civil-society organizations in education, health, agriculture, sports, tourism, technology, infrastructure, legal services and investment. Through initiatives such as Beyond Africa Now and the Brazil–Africa CEO Forum, it advances strategic communication, business matchmaking, economic diplomacy and socially inclusive projects.

GPS linkages

GPS can engage Beyond Africa as a Brazil–Africa bridge for investment, knowledge exchange, university and government partnerships, business matchmaking, and South–South cooperation across education, agriculture, health, technology, infrastructure and entrepreneurship.

03

Astra Landing Enterprise

Founder / Executive: Sajith Kaimal
Base / Reach: Massachusetts, United States · Global sourcing and markets

Leadership profile

A technology and business executive with experience spanning cloud computing, artificial intelligence, logistics and global supply chains. He founded Astra Landing Enterprise to connect producers and farmers with international markets through responsible sourcing and technology-enabled trade.

Principal activities

Astra Landing specializes in premium food importing, global sourcing, agricultural supply chains and international distribution. Its objectives include ethical and sustainable sourcing, improved market access for producers, and technology-enabled efficiency, transparency and global connectivity in agriculture and food systems.

GPS linkages

GPS can connect Astra Landing to its Agriculture, Knowledge & Innovation, Entrepreneurship and Social Impact activities by supporting market-access partnerships, responsible sourcing, technology-enabled value chains, and links between Southern producers and international markets.

04

Kanaka Agro Farms

Founder / Executive: Sajith Kaimal
Base / Reach: Massachusetts, United States · Global sourcing and markets

Leadership profile

A technology-driven agricultural venture focused on premium saffron production.

Principal activities

Kanaka Agro Farms combines organic cultivation with artificial intelligence, robotics and vertical-farming systems. Its objective is to produce high-quality saffron sustainably while demonstrating how controlled-environment agriculture and automation can improve productivity, resource efficiency and access to specialized global food markets.

GPS linkages

GPS can position Kanaka Agro Farms within its Agriculture, Climate & Environment and Innovation agenda, using the venture as a platform for knowledge exchange on AI, robotics, controlled-environment agriculture, sustainability, productivity and specialized global markets.

05

inste^d

Founder / Executive: Adu Opoku-Boahin
Base / Reach: Switzerland and Ghana · Global development partnerships

Leadership profile

A technology and transformation leader with experience in consulting, analytics and organizational change. As CEO of inste^d, he leads an AI-powered development platform designed to help governments and organizations understand multidimensional poverty, target interventions and measure results more effectively.

Principal activities

inste^d combines Poverty Assessment AI, granular household and community data, intervention management, impact measurement and skills development. Its Skills Farm initiative in Ghana supports employable skills, entrepreneurship and digital workforce participation, complementing its broader objective of turning data and technology into targeted, measurable pathways out of poverty.

GPS linkages

GPS can link inste^d to its AI & Digital Innovation, Youth & Skills, Policy, and Social Impact activities through data-driven policy, poverty measurement, skills development, entrepreneurship, impact evaluation and scalable digital solutions.

06

Safe Gas Rwanda

Founder / Executive: Liban Mugabo
Base / Reach: Rwanda · Energy, LPG and strategic development

Leadership profile

A specialist in national-level strategic planning, research and finance, with strong professional experience across the public, private and NGO sectors. He holds two Master’s degrees, in Economics and Public Policy. He is currently Managing Director of Safe Gas Rwanda, a major LPG company in Rwanda. His previous experience includes Goldman Sachs, the Office of the President of Rwanda, the William J. Clinton Foundation’s HIV/AIDS Initiative, On the Frontier (OTF) Group, Rwanda Investment & Export Promotion Agency (RIEPA), and other private-sector and government offices.

Principal activities

Safe Gas operates in the LPG and energy sector, with a focus on the distribution and retail of cooking gas and on safer LPG solutions. Mugabo has also contributed regularly to public discussion on Rwanda’s socio-economic and energy-development priorities through strategic commentary in The New Times.

GPS linkages

GPS can connect Safe Gas to its Energy, Climate & Environment and Policy activities through dialogue on clean cooking, energy transition, private-sector innovation, national development strategy, research, and public–private approaches to sustainable energy access.

07

Pedro Coffee

Founder / Executive: Jean-Paul Rwagasana · Coffee Business Center Ltd. (CBC)
Base / Reach: Rwanda · Coffee production, roasting, value addition and markets

Leadership profile

Jean-Paul Rwagasana is a Rwandan entrepreneur, coffee-industry specialist and founder and Executive Chairman of Coffee Business Center Ltd., the company behind Pedro’s Coffee. With several decades of experience in Rwanda and Burundi’s coffee sector, he has expertise in coffee production, processing, quality control, dry milling and international trade.

Principal activities

Through Coffee Business Center, he works with coffee farmers and washing stations while overseeing the preparation and export of Rwandan coffee to international markets. His leadership has contributed to strengthening local value chains, promoting high-quality Rwandan coffee and expanding Rwanda’s presence in the global coffee industry. Pedro Coffee is positioned as a premium Rwandan coffee brand and contributes to the value chain by linking local production and processing with consumer and hospitality markets.

GPS linkages

GPS can connect Pedro Coffee to its Agriculture, Entrepreneurship, Knowledge & Innovation and Social Impact activities by highlighting value addition, farmer–market linkages, agribusiness knowledge, international market access, youth and enterprise development, and the global visibility of Rwandan products.
