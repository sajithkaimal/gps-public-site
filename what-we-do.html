Our Work

Four pillars. One ecosystem.

GPS generates knowledge, builds capacity, drives implementation, convenes actors, and mobilises partnerships — through four pillars that feed each other in every direction.

Pillar 01

Knowledge Co-Creation, Sharing & Dissemination

Research, universities, knowledge networks, publications, training, and fellowships — the South generating its own evidence and ideas.

Feeds every pillar
Pillar 02

Technology & Innovation

AI, digital transformation, innovation labs, entrepreneurship, smart agriculture, and health innovation — research becoming practical solutions.

Connects research to solutions
Pillar 03

Policy, Governance & Public Leadership

Public policy, governance, diplomacy, policy advisory, and leadership dialogues — knowledge and innovation reaching policymaking.

Turns evidence into decisions
Pillar 04

Enterprise & Sustainable Development

Private sector, industry, start-ups, investment, business partnerships, and executive education — enterprise connected to knowledge and impact.

Carries knowledge to market
The connections are the point

Interconnected, never linear.

At GPS, people work together across disciplines, sectors and regions, connecting knowledge, innovation, policy and enterprise so that they reinforce one another and transform ideas into action, impact and social transformation.
