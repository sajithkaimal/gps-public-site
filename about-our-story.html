About · Origins

Our Story

GPS did not begin with a building or a budget. It began with a question: what if the South's knowledge, institutions, and imagination were actually connected?

The idea behind GPS has deep roots — in a century of Southern cooperation frameworks, and in the everyday experience of researchers, policymakers, entrepreneurs, and artists whose work rarely found its way to one another.

In 2026, that idea took institutional form. Founding documents were adopted, a five-year strategic framework was set, and Kigali was chosen as headquarters — with regional hubs designed from day one across Africa, Latin America, and the Caribbean, and future hubs planned for the Middle East and Asia.

What follows is the arc we are building along. It will grow as GPS grows.

Timeline

From emerging platform to global institution.

2026 Foundation & ecosystem building

The platform takes form

Institutional structuring — governance model, advisory councils, legal structures. Digital launch of GPS. First university partnerships formalized. The GPS Dialogues series begins building intellectual momentum. Preparation of Kigali 2027.

2027 Global positioning

Kigali Knowledge Crossroads Week

GPS launches internationally as a major convening platform — built around the GCUB General Assembly and a multisectoral international conference. GPS Outlook, GPS Journal, and SouthPlus launch. GPS Academy and pilot innovation labs open.

2028 Implementation & expansion

Regional roots deepen

The São Paulo Convening extends GPS into Latin America. Regional hubs expand. The GPS Accelerator launches to translate ideas into scalable ventures. Policy advisory programs begin with ministries, universities, and cities.

São Paulo 2028GPS AcceleratorPolicy advisoryCommunity pilots
2029 Consolidation & impact

Networks become infrastructure

GPS Fellowship programs for researchers, policymakers, artists, and emerging leaders. Research-to-impact pilots demonstrate measurable outcomes. The Global South Data & Foresight Platform and the GPS Impact Observatory come online.

FellowshipsData & ForesightImpact ObservatoryDevelopment partners
2030–31 Systemic transformation

A permanent institution

The GPS Global Summit. Large-scale implementation programs — educational transformation, AI governance, climate adaptation, digital inclusion. The Global South Policy & Innovation Consortium creates permanent collaboration mechanisms.
