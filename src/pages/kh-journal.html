Aims & scope

The GPS Journal publishes research that crosses the platform's four pillars — knowledge generation, technology and innovation, enterprise, and policy and public leadership — with particular attention to plural knowledge systems, research-to-policy pathways, and locally grounded innovation.

Transdisciplinary and trans-regional submissions are the norm, not the exception. Every article carries a practitioner-facing abstract, because scholarship here expects an audience beyond the academy.

Publication model

  • Open access, no author fees for Global South-based scholars
  • Double-anonymous peer review
  • First issue: 2027, alongside the Kigali Crossroads Week

Submissions

How to submit

1 · Prepare your manuscript
6,000–9,000 words for research articles; 2,500–4,000 for policy-facing commentary. Any recognized citation style, applied consistently. Include a 150-word practitioner abstract.
2 · Anonymize for review
Remove identifying information from the manuscript file; include a separate title page with authors and affiliations.
3 · Submit by email (interim)
Until the submission system launches with the GPS Academy platform, send manuscripts to info@gpsouth.org with the CFP number in the subject line.
4 · Review & decision
Double-anonymous review by two referees; first decisions targeted within 10 weeks.
