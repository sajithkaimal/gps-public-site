GPS is a South-led global platform connecting people, knowledge, technology, policy, and enterprise to co-create and share solutions that advance social transformation across the Global South.

Who do we connect?

Universities, governments, businesses, civil society, innovators, researchers, artists, and communities.

What do we connect?

Knowledge, technology and innovation, policy, and enterprise — in every direction, never a straight line.

Why do we connect them?

To co-create solutions, opportunities, sustainable development, and societal impact across the Global South.

The GPS ecosystem

What makes the GPS model distinctive.

At GPS, people work together across disciplines, sectors and regions, connecting knowledge, innovation, policy and enterprise so that they reinforce one another and transform ideas into action, impact and social transformation.
