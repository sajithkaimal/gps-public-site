A strategic forum, not a ceremony

The General Assembly is a working forum for governance, planning, mobility, and international cooperation across GCUB — the Grupo de Cooperação Internacional de Universidades Brasileiras — and its partner institutions across the Global South.

Hosting the Assembly in Kigali is itself the point: Brazilian and African higher education leadership in one room, with the Crossroads Week's policy dialogues, innovation forums, and cultural programming around them.

What it produces

  • Academic mobility agreements and pilot schemes in priority disciplines
  • Joint supervision and tuition-cooperation models
  • Collaborative research agendas across the Atlantic
  • Institutional MoUs signed during the Week
