7 Institutional commitment

By submitting this application, the institution expresses its intention to:

  • Support GPS's mission as a South-led, transdisciplinary and trans-sectoral platform.
  • Participate in good faith in relevant GPS programmes, networks and collaborative initiatives.
  • Designate an institutional focal point and contribute, according to its capacities, to mutually agreed activities.
  • Promote reciprocal opportunities for students, academics, researchers, innovators and institutional partners.
  • Respect applicable laws, academic standards, research ethics, confidentiality, data protection and intellectual property.
  • Promote equality, inclusion and non-discrimination.
  • Recognize that membership is non-exclusive and that specific financial or legally binding commitments require separate written agreement.
