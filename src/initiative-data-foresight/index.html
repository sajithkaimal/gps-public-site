Initiatives · Data platform · From 2029

Global South Data & Foresight

Trends, scenarios, and strategic intelligence — built for Southern planners, by Southern institutions.

Component

Dashboards

Policy dashboards on education, health, climate, food systems, and digital transformation.

Component

Analytics

Data systems and analytics services for governments and universities in the network.

Component

Scenario building

Foresight tools for exploring futures — demographic, technological, climatic, geopolitical.

Component

Policy intelligence

Strategic monitoring supporting ministries, hubs, and the Impact Observatory.

A long-term strategic feature

The Platform is scheduled for development from 2029, building on data foundations laid by the innovation labs and the Impact Observatory. Like everything future-facing on this site, its structure exists now and activates progressively — sample indicator panels will appear here as pilots produce data.
