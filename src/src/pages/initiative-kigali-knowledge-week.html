Purpose

Not a conference. A catalytic moment.

In October 2027, GCUB and its member institutions gather in Kigali for their General Assembly — and around it, GPS convenes its flagship global gathering: universities, policymakers, public institutions, entrepreneurs, artists, and civil society from five continents.

The Week is deliberately designed as an action-oriented platform where research informs policy, education responds to societal needs, ideas become actionable solutions, and partnerships generate measurable social and economic impact.

At a time when education systems, labor markets, emerging technologies — especially AI — and global power relations are being reshaped, the Crossroads responds to an urgent need: realigning learning, research, and public action.

Photography — Kigali Convention Centre at dusk

Knowledge should function as a social contract — linking universities to society, research to decision-making, education to community aspirations.

The Week

Three components, one architecture.

Component 1

GCUB General Assembly

A strategic forum for presidents, rectors, and institutional leaders — governance, planning, mobility, and international cooperation. Dedicated page

Component 2

Multisectoral International Conference

Researchers, private-sector actors, Indigenous knowledge holders, policymakers, and artists. Academic papers peer-reviewed; other contributions expert-curated.

Component 3

The Crossroads Platform

Thematic panels and workshops · policy dialogues and university–government roundtables · education reform sessions · entrepreneurship and innovation forums · partnership meetings and MoU signings · youth engagement · exhibitions and cultural programming.

Strategic pillars

Five thematic tracks.

From Knowledge → Education & Methods → Policy → Partnerships → Impact.

I

Pedagogy & Curriculum Development

Transforming education for lived realities and future societies — experiential learning, critical thinking, and curricula connected to real-world problem-solving.

II

Technology & Digital Innovation

Reconfiguring knowledge ecosystems in the digital age — AI and emerging technologies in education and research, inclusive and ethical by design.

III

Epistemology & Knowledge Systems

Plural knowledge systems — indigenous, endogenous, and global epistemologies, toward more balanced global knowledge architectures.

IV

Applied, Impactful Teaching & Research

Research for societal challenges — transdisciplinary innovation labs, social sciences in action, and frameworks for measuring social impact.

V

Research Funding & Resource Mobilization

Knowledge partnerships connecting universities, policy, and society — sustainable financing for research and innovation ecosystems.
