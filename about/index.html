About GPS

A platform built to connect the South.

The Global Platform for the South (GPS) is a transdisciplinary research ecosystem, policy platform, and convening institution — advancing applied knowledge and innovation for social transformation across the Global South.

2026

Founded — headquartered in Kigali, Rwanda

6

Regional hubs across Africa, Latin America & the Caribbean

4

Interconnected pillars, one ecosystem

85%

of humanity lives in the Global South — and sets too little of the agenda
